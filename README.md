### MDA Analysis

**M1 — Numbered Cipher System**

- **Mechanic:** Each numbered cipher represents a letter. Players select letters to complete hidden words.
- **Dynamic:** Players compare numbers and letters to identify patterns and decode words.
- **Aesthetic:** Challenge — Players use logic and problem-solving skills.
- **Bartle Type:** Explorer — Players interact with the puzzle world to discover its rules.

**M2 — Mistake Counter**

- **Mechanic:** Selecting an incorrect letter adds a cross to the mistake counter.
- **Dynamic:** Players become more careful and double-check their choices.
- **Aesthetic:** Challenge — Mistakes create pressure and make correct answers more rewarding.
- **Bartle Type:** Achiever — Players try to complete the puzzle while avoiding mistakes.

**M3 — Hammer Hint**

- **Mechanic:** The hammer hint reveals one hidden letter.
- **Dynamic:** Players use the revealed letter to understand the remaining word pattern.
- **Aesthetic:** Discovery — A small clue helps players discover the correct answer.
- **Bartle Type:** Explorer — Players use information to understand the puzzle.

**M4 — Star Hint**

- **Mechanic:** The star hint reveals an entire hidden word.
- **Dynamic:** Players use this help when they cannot solve a word independently.
- **Aesthetic:** Submission — Optional assistance allows players to continue at their own pace.
- **Bartle Type:** Achiever — Players use available assistance to complete the level.

**M5 — Level Progression**

- **Mechanic:** Completing all required words unlocks the next level.
- **Dynamic:** Players focus on finishing the current puzzle to progress.
- **Aesthetic:** Challenge — Completing a puzzle provides a sense of achievement.
- **Bartle Type:** Achiever — Players aim to progress through levels.

**M6 — Context-Based Words**

- **Mechanic:** Some words appear with question marks instead of cipher numbers and must be guessed from context.
- **Dynamic:** Players use the meaning of other words and their knowledge to predict the missing word.
- **Aesthetic:** Discovery — Players uncover words by interpreting clues and context.
- **Bartle Type:** Explorer — Players investigate clues to discover hidden answers.

**M7 — Locked Words**

- **Mechanic:** Some words remain locked until specific letters are found elsewhere. After unlocking, their cipher numbers appear, but the letters still need to be guessed.
- **Dynamic:** Players solve other words first, then return to the newly unlocked word.
- **Aesthetic:** Discovery — Unlocking new information encourages players to explore connections.
- **Bartle Type:** Explorer — Players discover new information by interacting with the puzzle system.

An Elmwood Trail is a mystery detective game in which players investigate a disappearance by exploring clues and solving puzzles.

### MDA Analysis

**M1 — Messages and Conversations**

- **Mechanic:** Players can access messages and conversations on characters' phones.
- **Dynamic:** Players read conversations and search for information that may help solve the mystery.
- **Aesthetic:** Discovery — Players uncover hidden information through messages.
- **Bartle Type:** Explorer — Players investigate the game's digital world.

**M2 — Digital Evidence**

- **Mechanic:** Players can access photos, social media, voicemails, and call records.
- **Dynamic:** Players examine different information sources and compare details.
- **Aesthetic:** Discovery — Exploring information reveals new clues.
- **Bartle Type:** Explorer — Players search the game world for information.

**M3 — Character Dialogue**

- **Mechanic:** Players interact with characters through dialogue and questioning.
- **Dynamic:** Players examine responses and look for inconsistencies.
- **Aesthetic:** Narrative — Conversations reveal character backgrounds and move the story forward.
- **Bartle Type:** Explorer — Players interact with characters to discover information.

**M4 — Puzzle Solving**

- **Mechanic:** The game includes puzzle-solving and code-breaking missions.
- **Dynamic:** Players analyse clues, recognise patterns, and solve puzzles to continue the investigation.
- **Aesthetic:** Challenge — Puzzles test logic and problem-solving skills.
- **Bartle Type:** Achiever — Players overcome challenges to progress.

**M5 — Clue Collection**

- **Mechanic:** Players collect clues and unlock information related to the missing girl.
- **Dynamic:** Players connect newly discovered information with earlier events.
- **Aesthetic:** Narrative — Evidence gradually reveals the events behind the mystery.
- **Bartle Type:** Explorer — Players discover the hidden story through evidence.

**M6 — Suspect Investigation**

- **Mechanic:** Players can connect related clues and people using the investigation system.
- **Dynamic:** Players organise evidence, compare relationships, and develop theories.
- **Aesthetic:** Discovery — Connecting clues helps players uncover relationships.
- **Bartle Type:** Explorer — Players explore connections within the game world.

**M7 — Hint System**

- **Mechanic:** Players can use hints when they are unable to solve an objective.
- **Dynamic:** Players use available assistance and continue the investigation.
- **Aesthetic:** Submission — Optional assistance allows players to continue at their own pace.
- **Bartle Type:** Achiever — Players use available help to complete objectives.

## Overall Aesthetic Profile

### Code Busters

The main aesthetics are **Challenge** and **Discovery**. Challenge comes from solving cipher puzzles and avoiding mistakes. Discovery comes from unlocking hidden words and finding clues.

### An Elmwood Trail

The main aesthetics are **Discovery, Narrative, and Challenge**. Players discover clues, follow the mystery's story, and solve puzzles during the investigation.

---

## Overall Bartle Player Types

Bartle's player types are divided into four categories:

- **Achievers (Acting on World):** Players who focus on completing goals and achieving success.
- **Explorers (Interacting with World):** Players who investigate and discover things in the game world.
- **Socializers (Interacting with Players):** Players who enjoy interacting with other players.
- **Killers (Acting on Players):** Players who enjoy competing against or acting on other players.

### Code Busters

The primary player type is **Explorer** because players investigate clues and discover hidden words. The secondary type is **Achiever** because players aim to complete puzzles and unlock new levels.

### An Elmwood Trail

The primary player type is **Explorer** because players investigate evidence and discover information. The secondary type is **Achiever** because players solve puzzles and complete investigation objectives.
